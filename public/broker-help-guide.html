15D Wings Broker Quick Start Guide

Welcome to 15D Wings. This simple guide explains everything you need to know to book private flights, invite airline operators, and manage your commissions seamlessly.

Need Assistance? Contact our 24/7 executive concierge and compliance desk anytime at support@15dwings.com.ng.

1. Logging In & Creating Your Account

Access your secure broker portal using your registered email address. When you sign up or sign in, our system instantly configures your secure broker identification profile and connects your unique referral code.

2. Inviting Partner Airline Operators

Step-by-Step Operator Onboarding:
  • Open the Operator Onboarding & Network tab in your dashboard.
  • Copy your unique operator invite link pointing to airlines.15dwings.com.ng.
  • Send the link to your partner airline operator. When they register using your invite, their fleet is automatically attributed to your broker ID in our database for transparent commission tracking.

3. Booking Flight Missions & Managing Escrow

Flight Missions & VIP Escrow (vip.15dwings.com.ng):
  • Use the Flight Mission & Escrow tab to view live flight missions.
  • Enter your Mission ID and client email to securely query escrow payment status and deposit verifications directly from our secure database.
  • All mission bookings and commissions are recorded automatically against your verified broker profile.

4. Checking Your Earnings & Commissions

Your dashboard features a real-time commission ledger. Whenever your connected flights are confirmed or completed, your 10% platform commission is automatically calculated and credited to your pending settlement ledger.
